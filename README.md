Food Ordering Behaviour and Consumer Trends
📊 Project Overview

Food Ordering Behaviour and Consumer Trends is a Tableau-based data visualization project that analyzes customer food-ordering patterns and consumer preferences. The project transforms food-ordering data into interactive dashboards and a Tableau Story to identify important trends related to customers, cities, cuisines, meal types, discounts, mood, hunger levels, and ordering behaviour.

The goal is to provide clear and interactive visual insights that can help understand customer behaviour and support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer food-ordering behaviour.
Identify popular cuisines and meal types.
Understand customer age distribution.
Identify the top cities by order volume.
Compare weekday and weekend ordering patterns.
Analyze the effect of discounts on orders.
Understand customer mood and satisfaction patterns.
Analyze cuisine preferences across different cities.
Study the relationship between hunger level and average order value.
Present key findings through an interactive Tableau Dashboard and Story.
📌 Business Problem

Food-ordering businesses generate large amounts of customer and order data. However, analyzing raw data manually makes it difficult to identify customer preferences, popular cuisines, ordering patterns, and factors affecting customer spending.

This project uses Tableau to convert the available food-ordering data into meaningful visualizations, dashboards, and a story that make these patterns easier to understand.

📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard and Tableau Story include the following analyses:

1. Customer Age Distribution

Analyzes customers across different age groups to identify the major customer segments.

2. Meal Type Analysis

Compares orders across different meal types to identify customers' preferred meal periods.

3. Top 5 Cities by Order Volume

Identifies the five cities generating the highest number of food orders.

4. Weekday vs Weekend Analysis

Compares customer ordering behaviour between weekdays and weekends.

5. Discount Analysis

Analyzes customer orders based on discount usage to understand promotional behaviour.

6. Customer Mood Analysis

Examines customer mood patterns to understand the customer experience.

7. City vs Cuisine Analysis

Compares cuisine preferences across cities to identify location-based food preferences.

8. Average Order by Hunger Level

Analyzes average order value across different hunger levels to understand spending behaviour.

🔍 Key Insights
Customer ordering behaviour varies across different age groups.
Meal preferences differ based on the type of meal period.
A small number of cities contribute a significant share of overall order volume.
Cuisine preferences vary across different cities.

📖 Tableau Story Flow

The Tableau Story presents the analysis in the following sequence:

Customer Age Distribution → Meal Type Analysis → Top 5 Cities → Weekday vs Weekend → Discount Analysis → Customer Mood → City vs Cuisine → Average Order by Hunger Level

This flow helps users move from customer demographics to ordering behaviour, location preferences, and finally customer spending behaviour.
